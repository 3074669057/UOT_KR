\section*{Appendix B: The Adequacy Criterion (Long-Form Rationale)}

The gate $G \ge 8$ is not a universal statistical threshold. $G$ counts independent primary-address clusters---a proxy for behavioral-source diversity, not sample count: 2{,}451 source-level fan-out units concentrated in seven clusters cannot be treated as 2{,}451 independent external actors. The complete frozen rule has four jointly prespecified criteria ($G \ge 8$; maximum single-cluster share $< 0.5$; Tier-A source-level fan-out units $\ge 30$; temporal spread $\ge 2$ calendar months); one failed criterion fails the gate. Its scientific value comes from pre-specification plus conservative diversity control, not from any property of the number eight. After observing $G = 7$, relaxing the criterion to seven would have been a post-data change to a confirmatory adequacy rule, and no method prediction existed at the time adequacy failed, so the failure decision cannot have been influenced by any method outcome. The dataset itself is a fully verified, disjoint, prediction-blind artifact: DATA / GT / PROVENANCE / DISJOINTNESS all PASS; only the preregistered external-performance inference is not evaluable. The external-validation experiment line is closed for this submission (no v5).

\section*{Appendix C: Metric Definitions}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Edge-inclusion recall.} A ground-truth edge counts as recovered iff the decoded plan assigns it positive mass ($\ge$1e-9)---inclusion over the dense decoded plan, not exact topology recovery.
\item \textbf{Per-source Recall@3.} Macro-averaged fraction of source flows---with at least one GT destination and one decoded edge $>$1e-9---whose GT destination appears among the top-3 destinations by descending decoded mass, averaged over seeds 42--46. Rank-based inclusion; tie-break implementation-dependent (unstable sort).
\item \textbf{Strict exact recovery.} The predicted edge set must equal the structural ground truth exactly; one extra edge forfeits the template.
\item \textbf{tx-CVR.} Fraction of accepted decoded pairs with negative per-transaction delay.
\item \textbf{Coverage / abstention.} Coverage: source flows with a non-abstained tx projection (denominator: source flows in the plan). Abstention: labeled anchor pairs with abstained decoding (denominator: 7{,}296). Denominators differ by design.
\item \textbf{Paired hierarchical CI.} Within-bridge template-level paired resample ($B=4{,}000$, \texttt{RandomState(20240101)}); replicate statistic $=$ unweighted mean of per-bridge paired means; the seed level enters the point estimate but not the resampling.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Appendix G: Full Derivation of Proposition 1 and Zero-Support Repair}

\textbf{Full proof of Proposition 1.} Let $P=\mathrm{diag}(u)K\mathrm{diag}(v)$ with $u_i>0$, $v_j>0$, $K_{ij}>0$. Then $c_j=\sum_l P_{lj}=\sum_l u_l K_{lj} v_j=v_j\sum_l u_l K_{lj}$, and $c_j>0$ implies $v_j\sum_l u_l K_{lj}>0$; hence $S^{\mathrm{row}}_{ij}=P_{ij}/c_j=u_i K_{ij}v_j/(v_j\sum_l u_l K_{lj})=u_i K_{ij}/\sum_l u_l K_{lj}$, which is independent of $v$. Symmetrically, $r_i=\sum_l P_{il}=u_i\sum_l K_{il}v_l$, so $S^{\mathrm{col}}_{ij}=P_{ij}/r_i=u_i K_{ij}v_j/(u_i\sum_l K_{il}v_l)=K_{ij}v_j/\sum_l K_{il}v_l$, which is independent of $u$. Gauge invariance: for any positive rescaling $\tilde v=\alpha v$, $\tilde c_j=\alpha c_j$ and $\tilde P_{ij}=\alpha P_{ij}$, so $S^{\mathrm{row}}$ is unchanged (symmetrically for $u$ and $S^{\mathrm{col}}$); $P$-based ranking is not invariant under such rescalings of the ranked-against side. The identity uses only the factorization, nonnegativity, and division at positive-mass endpoints; it holds for balanced entropic OT ($P=\mathrm{diag}(a)K\mathrm{diag}(b)$) and entropic unbalanced OT with KL penalties alike, and says nothing about which solver family produces a better plan. Interpreting the conditional score as a kernel ranking requires the decoder to read the plan at the solver's $\varepsilon$ (both frozen at 0.05); the frozen plans satisfy the factorization to solver tolerance (final residuals $\le 10^{-11}$, verified per cell).

\textbf{Zero-support repair (mathematical basis and tests).} The KL-unbalanced objective assigns $+\infty$ cost to placing positive mass on a zero-reference row or column, so restricting the problem to the positive-marginal support ($I_+\times J_+$) cannot change the optimum; an all-zero side maps to an all-zero plan. Empirical equivalence (non-test data only): POSITIVE\_SUPPORT\_EQUIVALENCE 15/15 development cells PASS (0.0 plan difference, 0 decoder discrepancies); ZERO\_SUPPORT\_TESTS 8/8 PASS.

\section*{Appendix I: Audited Causes of the All-Zero Strict-Exact Column}

(i) The frozen 1e-9 decode threshold is a ``positive mass'' rule and both entropic plans are dense (18{,}776 edges on the Celer seed-42 grid; balanced plan $\sim$30{,}000), so strict equality with a 6-edge truth is unattainable. (ii) The true structural edges are not the locally cheapest cells (per-source cost rank $\approx$3--4; Top-$k$-MM edge F1 rises from 0.019 at Top-2 to 0.248 at Top-3) because the fan-out source's cheapest target is the full-amount merge destination and vice versa, so any local threshold that keeps the truth edges also keeps cheaper confusers, and the transport methods include the truth edges only through global mass allocation. (iii) The legacy metric counts inclusion, not exactness. These are audited causes, not evaluator artifacts (see the structural-mechanism diagnostics in the package).
