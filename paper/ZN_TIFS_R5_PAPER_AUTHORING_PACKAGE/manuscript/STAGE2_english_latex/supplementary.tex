\section*{Appendix D: Metric Definitions}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Edge-inclusion recall (Tables II/III).} A ground-truth edge counts as recovered iff the decoded transport plan assigns it positive mass ($\ge$1e-9). This is inclusion over the dense decoded plan, not exact topology recovery.
\item \textbf{Per-source Recall@3 (Table II).} Macro-averaged fraction of source flows---among those with at least one ground-truth destination and at least one decoded edge with transport mass $>$1e-9---for which at least one ground-truth destination appears among the top-3 destinations ranked by descending decoded transport mass, averaged over seeds 42--46. Rank-based inclusion; not strict recovery; per-source macro; tie-break is implementation-dependent (unstable sort).
\item \textbf{Strict exact recovery (Table V).} A template is recovered iff the predicted edge set equals its structural ground truth exactly; one extra edge forfeits the template.
\item \textbf{tx-CVR.} Temporal-causality violation rate: fraction of accepted decoded pairs whose per-transaction delay is negative.
\item \textbf{Coverage / abstention (Table VIII).} Coverage: fraction of source flows with at least one non-abstained tx projection (denominator: source flows in the transport plan). Abstention rate: fraction of labeled anchor pairs for which decoding abstains (denominator: 7{,}296). Denominators differ by design.
\item \textbf{Paired hierarchical CI (Table VI).} Within-bridge template-level paired resample ($B=4{,}000$, \texttt{RandomState(20240101)}); replicate statistic is the unweighted mean of per-bridge paired means; the seed level enters the point estimate but not the resampling.
\end{itemize}
