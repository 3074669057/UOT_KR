\subsubsection{Why Unbalanced Transport (Mechanism Ladders)}
\label{sec:whyunbalanced}

Four seed-controlled ladders (seeds 101--103, identical cost matrices, no re-tuning) isolate the unbalanced mechanism. Clean conditions: at 0\% mass mismatch, Balanced-OT is not close to RC-UOT-Q (edge F1 0.070 vs.\ 0.110 Celer; 0.032 vs.\ 0.047 Multichain; 0.033 vs.\ 0.045 PolyNetwork)---shedding mass on confusers matters even at the balanced point. Under mass mismatch both transport methods degrade (Celer precision 0.043$\to$0.024 balanced; 0.068$\to$0.037 RC-UOT-Q) while Threshold-MM is flat or improves (0.142$\to$0.155); RC-UOT-Q stays above Balanced-OT at every level as a level offset, not a slope difference. Under unmatched ratios, all methods lose precision while RC-UOT-Q destroys the excess mass (its transported mass falls---the explicit abstention signal). Under decoy density, Threshold-MM's pairwise-independent false positives grow fastest (26.3$\to$223.6 FP per template at $k{=}8$, $+$750\%; precision 0.182$\to$0.061) vs.\ slower joint-method growth ($+$278\%/$+$239\%); timestamp noise ($\le$180-s) does not discriminate the methods. The data therefore support the weakest preregistered conclusion: RC-UOT-Q removes the representational limitation of one-to-one hard matching; they do not support global-transport consistency superiority over the calibrated local rule, and unbalanced mass robustness is supported only as a consistent edge-metric level offset, not exact-topology robustness (full ladder numbers in Supplement Table S6).

\subsubsection{Confirmatory Holdout: Conditional-Plan Decoding Repairs Raw-Plan Correspondence Ranking}
\label{sec:confirmatory}

On development seeds 201--205, amount-free cost decoding attained macro edge F1 0.3172 while raw-plan mutual-top-5 decoding (RAW\_UOT\_PLAN\_D4) attained only 0.2374; the diagnosis traced the gap to marginal competition and destination-side dual scaling. We preregistered a dual-cancelled conditional decoder ($S^{\mathrm{row}}=P_{ij}/c_j$, $S^{\mathrm{col}}=P_{ij}/r_i$, mutual top-5, $k{=}5$) with a five-gate decision tree before any holdout data existed. All components---amount-free cost, risk/evidence-weighted marginals, reg $=0.05$, reg\_m $=0.5$, decoder, paired bootstrap ($B=4{,}000$, \texttt{RandomState(20240101)}), decision rules---were hash-locked before the untouched seeds 301--305 (three bridges, 48 templates per seed, 720 paired template instances) were generated or read; the one-shot runner verifies every hash, and an independent verification pass recomputes all results from raw per-cell artifacts within 1e-9.

\textbf{Primary result.} RAW\_UOT\_PLAN\_D4 attains macro edge F1 0.2350 and the conditional decoder 0.3104: \textbf{$\Delta_{\mathrm{primary}}=+0.075413$, paired 95\% CI $[0.070557,0.080312]$}, entirely above zero (gate A; Table~\ref{tab:confirmatory}; Fig.~\ref{fig:confirmatory}). The CI is a within-bridge template-level paired resample (the seed level enters the point estimate but not the resampling); per-bridge paired s.d. 0.0704/0.0881/0.0247. The improvement is positive on every bridge---Celer $+0.0826$ $[0.0743,0.0918]$, Multichain $+0.0820$ $[0.0709,0.0933]$, PolyNetwork $+0.0616$ $[0.0587,0.0649]$ (gate D)---so the primary effect is not driven by a single bridge.

\textbf{Mechanism gate (B).} The exact preregistered directional signature holds: harmful-flip rates decrease by $-0.2595$ (row), $-0.2303$ (column), $-0.5360$ (fan-out child), $-0.5095$ (merge destination), and GT mutual-top5 retention increases by $+0.2035$. These observational indicators are consistent with the ranking-repair mechanism, not a complete solver-level causal decomposition. \textbf{Recovery (gate C).} With $D=+0.070569$, recovery\_fraction $=1.0686$ (point estimate; the amount-free cost reference was a reference, not a ceiling).

\textbf{Transport-plan contribution and UOT/BOT.} Conditional decoding of the transport plan exceeds support-plus-kernel decoding ($\Delta_{\mathrm{support}}=+0.0104$ $[0.0062,0.0152]$) and amount-free cost decoding ($\Delta_{\mathrm{cost}}=+0.0048$ $[0.0013,0.0089]$)---positive transport-plan-level contribution at the macro level (TYPE A; PolyNetwork's secondary contrasts are negative: $\Delta_{\mathrm{cost}}=-0.0043$ $[-0.0065,-0.0024]$, $\Delta_{\mathrm{support}}=-0.0043$ $[-0.0065,-0.0024]$, so TYPE A is a three-bridge macro classification, not bridge-uniform). This is not a claim about the unbalanced relaxation: CONDITIONAL\_BOT\_D4 (0.3106) and CONDITIONAL\_UOT\_D4 (0.3104) differ by $\Delta_{\mathrm{bot}}\approx-0.0003$ (unrounded macro mean $-0.0002688$; 95\% CI $[-0.0018,+0.0012]$, includes zero), so \textbf{no unbalanced-relaxation-specific benefit is established}. Integrity: FP/template 1.0511, FN/template 0.4945 (within the 3$\times$ bound), max solver residuals $\le 10^{-11}$, zero NaNs/failures/duplicates.

\textbf{Limitations.} The endpoint is template-level edge F1 on semi-synthetic structure (strict exact-topology recovery remains zero; Table~\ref{tab:strict}); $k=5$; mechanism indicators are observational.

\subsection{Fixed-Delay RC-UOT-Q on Full Anchor Pairs}
\label{sec:fixeddelay}

Using the \texttt{tx\_if\_available\_else\_flow\_representative} delay policy, RC-UOT is evaluated on all \textbf{7{,}296} Celer anchor pairs as a ranked flow-correspondence model; the frozen plan is \emph{not re-solved}---admissibility applies decode-only temporal masks and top-$k$ rescue (Table~\ref{tab:fixeddelay}). The raw tx-level argmax projection (pair F1 0.589, top-3 recall 0.658) is a \emph{compatibility projection} only (tx-level CVR 0.338), not a forensic admissibility claim. The formal high-coverage decoding (\texttt{positive\_delay\_top3\_rescue}) reaches pair F1 0.590 with coverage 0.996 and tx-CVR 0.005; the forensic high-confidence subset (\texttt{joint\_time\_admissible\_filter}) yields precision \textbf{0.889} and pair F1 \textbf{0.708} at coverage \textbf{0.845} with tx-CVR \textbf{0}. A permuted-label control collapses pair P/R/F1 to near zero. Coverage (source flows with a non-abstained projection) and abstention (all 7{,}296 pairs) denominators differ by design. The fixed-delay leave-anchor-out audit shows \texttt{forbidden\_features\_remaining} $=0$ with fake-anchor probes neutralized, and baseline/leave-key-out/strict agree within 0.02---the gains are not explained by bridge-key or bridge-evidence leakage (Supplement Table S7).

\subsubsection{Symmetric Masking and Baseline Applicability}
\label{sec:maskingladder}

Under a symmetric masking ladder on the shared 7{,}296 tx-pair ground truth and BNB candidate universe (Supplement Table S3), original Connector (\texttt{WithdrawLocator} raw top-1) reaches F1 \textbf{0.9736} under full native bridge semantics and the closed-set candidate pool---an upper-bound native-feature diagnostic, since the pool equals the 7{,}296 GT destination hashes; RC-UOT-Q (joint filter) reaches \textbf{0.7085}. Removing receiver or amount semantics blocks or collapses Connector (receiver: \texttt{BLOCKED\_BY\_REQUIRED\_RECEIVER\_SEMANTICS}, N/A; amount: zero predictions, F1 0.0000), while RC-UOT-Q degrades (0.7001/0.3714/0.3752) but remains evaluable with tx-CVR $=0$ throughout. \textbf{The contribution is not that RC-UOT-Q dominates Connector in native mode:} RC-UOT-Q provides a ranked flow-correspondence model with an admissible abstaining decoder that stays applicable when bridge-specific matching semantics are absent. Route A v1 (inflated F1 0.9953) was rejected for decimal-bootstrap inconsistency; ABCTracer remains blocked (no official checkpoint).

\subsection{Coverage-Qualified Quotient Inference}
\label{sec:coveredquotient}

On the \textbf{122} covered holdout pairs (44/78; seeds 212--231), the development-frozen bridge-transfer-key rule reproduces the transferId-consistent quotient grouping exactly (P/R/F1 $=$ 1.000; Supplement Table S1). This is a \textbf{definitional consistency check} on the same protocol-native transferId association rule---not independent-accuracy and not external-performance evidence. Event-backed projection coverage is $\approx$\textbf{0.792} (gate-reference seeds 52--57; the full-scope gate fails on this value), so covered metrics must not be read as full-population recovery.

\subsection{Comparison with Cross-Chain Tracing Baselines}
\label{sec:baselines}

RC-UOT-Q (precision-oriented reranking, development-frozen) is compared with heuristic/style-adapted diagnostic baselines---\emph{not} original-system runs---on an independent development-sealed flow-stress holdout (Table~\ref{tab:baselines}). \textbf{Calibration asymmetry disclosed:} RC-UOT-Q received a development tuning budget (logistic precision reranker trained on dev seeds 52--71; threshold $\tau=0.7796$ selected on dev; decoder selected on dev; all frozen before holdout 292--311 was touched), whereas the two adapted baselines have zero calibration budget (author-set fixed constants in the frozen scripts/config catalog, \texttt{calibration="none"}, threshold 0.0, top-k 50, no development-selection step; the repository contains no author statement that these constants were never implicitly adjusted, so their historical tuning status is stated at its most conservative level). The budget is \textbf{asymmetric and favors the proposed method}; Table~\ref{tab:baselines} is therefore a fixed-scope diagnostic with untuned heuristic controls---diagnostic/structural/native-semantic controls, not a budget-matched leaderboard, and not evidence of calibrated superiority. RC-UOT-Q improves precision and F1 (0.192/0.316) over both baselines (0.113/0.195; 0.100/0.181) while the ABCTracer-inspired baseline retains higher recall (0.975 vs.\ 0.900); these are single-partition point estimates without CIs (exploratory, RQ4). \subsection{Prototype Validation and Summary}

\textbf{Cross AML} executes the evidence-to-report pipeline end-to-end in dry-run mode (replaying bundled evidence without RPC credentials): input parsing, evidence replay, flow construction, coverage qualification, RC-UOT-Q-style scoring, and report generation (RQ5). The experiments support six conclusions: \textbf{(1) Benchmark---}the Celer corpus provides a reproducible flow-level CSFFC setting (7{,}296 pairs; 7{,}128 labels). \textbf{(2) Structural transport---}RC-UOT expresses fan-out/merge in the edge-inclusion sense (0.946/0.967); strict exact-topology recovery is 0.000 for every method, and the calibrated threshold rule dominates edge F1 (Table~\ref{tab:strict}). \textbf{(3) Decoder repair---}the preregistered holdout confirms conditional decoding repairs raw-plan ranking ($+0.0754$ $[0.0706,0.0803]$, positive on every bridge, all mechanism directions satisfied). \textbf{(4) Full-anchor admissible decoding---}top-3 rescue preserves coverage 0.996 with tx-CVR 0.005; the joint filter yields precision 0.889/F1 0.708 at coverage 0.845 (Table~\ref{tab:fixeddelay}). \textbf{(5) Covered quotient and baselines---}the 122-pair consistency check, the masking applicability curve, and the Table~\ref{tab:baselines} precision/F1 improvement with its calibration caveat. \textbf{(6) Independent real-data audit---}2{,}451 Tier-A fan-out units over 359.98 days confirm problem validity outside the development corpus, with no method executed (Section~\ref{sec:v4audit}).

\subsection{Independent Temporal Data Audit (Problem Validity; No Method Performance)}
\label{sec:v4audit}

A disjoint post-development Celer corpus (window 2024-05-20T00:00:00Z--2025-05-19T00:00:00Z; protocol-native linkage \texttt{Send.transferId == Relay.srcTransferId}; per-block continuity, independent GT verification, and disjointness against 359{,}256 historical transaction identities with zero intersections) was assembled data-only and prediction-blind, yielding \textbf{32{,}905} anchors, \textbf{25{,}621} unique flow-level GT edges, \textbf{2{,}451} Tier-A source-level fan-out units (degree $\le 5$: 2{,}425; degree $> 5$: 26), and \textbf{85} merge units over a \textbf{359.98-day} span (Table~\ref{tab:v4}). These units concentrated in \textbf{seven} independent primary-address clusters (910, 587, 452, 432, 60, 8, 2; max share \textbf{0.371277}), below the preregistered adequacy gate $G\ge 8$. Accordingly, no method predictions were generated and \textbf{no external-performance claim is made}; the audit is problem-validity evidence, and it is a different experiment from the 301--305 holdout.

\textbf{Why the gate is what it is.} $G$ counts independent primary-address clusters---a proxy for \emph{behavioral-source diversity}, not sample count: 2{,}451 fan-out units concentrated in seven clusters cannot be treated as 2{,}451 independent external actors. The gate was a \textbf{conservative, prespecified adequacy requirement, not a universal statistical threshold}, fixed before the corpus structure or any method outcome was observed (jointly with max share $<$0.5, Tier-A units $\ge$30, span $\ge$2 calendar months). Because v4 yielded $G=7$, the criterion was \emph{not} relaxed post hoc---that would be a post-data change to a confirmatory rule---and no method prediction existed when adequacy failed. Protocol-level Celer settlement is transaction-pairwise; non-one-to-one structure arises at the forensic flow level when settlements aggregate (the long-form justification is in Supplement Appendix~E).
